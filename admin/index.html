Post the monthly schedule

  1. Open Gemini, signed in with the Sage Hall Google account. Attach the flyer and paste this:
    Add each night on this flyer to my Sage Hall Dances calendar. Title: "Country Swing Dancing", or the event's name for special nights (like a fundraiser or club night). Location: exactly the venue name from the flyer, or leave it empty if there's no venue or it says TBD. Start at the time on the flyer. If no time is given, make it an all-day event; never guess a time. End at midnight for Fairgrounds and Mountain Valley Athletics nights, and 3 hours after the start for Cache Bar. Don't add anything that isn't on the flyer.
  2. Open the calendar and check the new nights against the flyer, especially any marked TBD. Those should be all-day events with no location.
  3. The website picks them up within a minute.

Then upload the same flyer under Upload the monthly flyer (backup copy). That keeps a copy of the schedule the website falls back to if Google Calendar can't be reached. It doesn't change what the website shows.

Add, change or cancel a dance night

  1. Tap Open the dance calendar and make sure the event is in the Sage Hall Dances calendar, not your personal one.
  2. Title it "Country Swing Dancing", or the event's name for a special night. Put the venue name in Location, spelled as on the website (for example "Fairgrounds Indoor Barn").
  3. If the time hasn't been announced, make it All day. It shows as Time TBA. If the place hasn't been announced, leave Location empty. It shows as Location TBA.
  4. To cancel a night, start its title with "Cancelled", e.g. "Cancelled: Country Swing Dancing". It stays on the website with a Cancelled tag. Use the Announcement banner in settings to explain why.

Staff who don't use the Sage Hall Google account need the calendar shared with them. In Google Calendar's settings for Sage Hall Dances, add them under Share with specific people with Make changes to events.

Dancers of the Week & this week's photos

  1. Tap Update Dancers of the Week & photos.
  2. Pick the week, type the dancers' names and a one- or two-sentence shout-out, and upload a photo. Photos straight from your phone are fine: the site shrinks them and removes location data automatically.
  3. Under This week's photos, delete last week's and add up to 12 new ones. Captions are optional.
  4. Hit Save.

Only feature people who are happy to be on the site. If someone asks for a photo to come down, delete it here and hit Save. Leave the names blank to show “first pick coming soon”.

Add an Instagram reel

  1. In Instagram, open the reel, tap Share, then Copy link.
  2. Tap Edit reels & venues and paste the link under Instagram reels.
  3. Put your favourite first. The top three show on the home page.

About the backup copy

The website reads the schedule from Google Calendar. If Google can't be reached, it shows the backup copy instead, which is filled in from the uploaded flyer. You can also correct it under Dance schedule in the editor. Edits there don't change the website while Google Calendar is working.
