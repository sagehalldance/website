Post the monthly schedule

  1. Tap Upload the monthly flyer, upload the same flyer image you post on Instagram, and hit Save.
  2. Within about five minutes, the website's schedule fills in that month's nights from the flyer. Nights marked TBD come in as Time TBA or Location TBA.
  3. Check the schedule on the website. If anything's off, fix it under Edit the dance schedule.

Only the flyer's month changes. If you re-upload a flyer, times and places you've already filled in by hand stay, unless the new flyer gives different ones. If the flyer can't be read clearly, the schedule stays as it was, so add the nights by hand instead.

Add a dance night

  1. Tap Edit the dance schedule and sign in with the email you were invited with.
  2. Scroll to the bottom of the list and tap Add an item.
  3. Pick the date, start time and venue. If the time or place hasn't been announced yet, choose Time TBA or Location TBA.
  4. For a special night (a fundraiser, a club night), add a title. Add a short note if there's anything else people should know.
  5. Hit Save. The website updates in about a minute.

Dancers of the Week & this week's photos

  1. Tap Update Dancers of the Week & photos.
  2. Pick the week, type the dancers' names and a one- or two-sentence shout-out, and upload a photo. Photos straight from your phone are fine: the site shrinks them and removes location data automatically.
  3. Under This week's photos, delete last week's and add up to 12 new ones. Captions are optional.
  4. Hit Save.

Only feature people who are happy to be on the site. If someone asks for a photo to come down, delete it here and hit Save. Leave the names blank to show “first pick coming soon”.

Add an Instagram reel

  1. In Instagram, open the reel, tap Share, then Copy link.
  2. Tap Edit reels, venues & FAQ and paste the link under Instagram reels.
  3. Put your favourite first. The top three show on the home page.

Cancelling a night

Open the night in the schedule and turn on Cancelled. It stays on the calendar with a “Cancelled” tag so people who saw the flyer know. Use the Announcement banner in settings to explain why.

Using Google Calendar instead?

If the site is connected to Google Calendar, skip the editor: add, move or cancel dances in Google Calendar and the website picks them up automatically. Put the venue name in the event’s location, and start the title with “Cancelled” to mark a night as cancelled.
